About me

I'm Lorena Riveira, language instructor. I moved to Amsterdam from Argentina. I'm a qualified CELTA teacher and I also trained as a language teacher in Buenos Aires. I would love to help you improve your Spanish or English.

I have spent more than fourteen years working as a teacher of teenagers and adults at all kinds of levels. My teaching approach is centred around creating a personalised and enjoyable learning experience. I love adapting my lessons to each student's individual needs, taking into account their age, their interests and the pace at which they learn. That way everyone can get the most out of their lessons and the best results.

In an increasingly connected world, speaking more than one language has become an essential skill for personal and professional success. I offer a rich and effective learning experience that will take you to your language goals.

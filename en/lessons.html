How the lessons work

Lessons built around you: your level, your goals and your pace.

  • Communicative approach
  • Personalised programmes
  • Online and in person
  • All levels
  • CELTA-qualified teacher
  • More than 14 years of experience

  • Qualified and experienced

    I am passionate about languages and experienced at every level. My commitment is to your success. I trained in language teaching, which is your guarantee of high-quality learning. With more than 14 years of experience, I am ready to thrive in a wide variety of situations and settings.

  • Technology at the cutting edge

    I keep myself at the forefront of digital education. My virtual lessons are taught in an interactive, lively online environment, using innovative digital tools and resources that make the most of your learning and give you flexibility over when and where you study.

  • Communicative approach

    I believe in the power of effective communication. My teaching approach centres on developing your speaking and listening skills, giving you the confidence and fluency to interact in real-life situations. Some examples of activities in my lessons include role plays, presentations, discussions, and debates.

  • Flexibility

    I understand that work and personal life can get in the way of your availability. Whatever your situation, we can adapt to it so that you always have the chance to carry on with your course. Whether that means adjusting the schedule week by week, or taking the lessons online, in person, or a mix of both.

  • Personalised programmes

    I know that every student has individual needs and goals. I adapt my study programmes to meet your specific requirements, whether you want to learn a new language from scratch, improve the skills you already have for the job market, begin a new life in another country, or prepare for international exams.

  • Materials

    In my programme I give you every resource you need to pick up the language and practise it comfortably from home. You get access to materials you can use both in print and in a digital version. On top of that, you have a wide selection of interactive exercises to keep your progress and your commitment to learning on track.
